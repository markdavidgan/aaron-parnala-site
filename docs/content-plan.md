# Content & Narrative Plan

## Page Hierarchy & Architecture

### 1. Home (`/`)
1. **Hero Section:** Full-bleed cinematic photography of Skinnovia Clinic reception with minimal headline: *"Spaces resolved with purpose."*
2. **Positioning Narrative:** Explaining the practice's focus on design + turnkey execution.
3. **Selected Works:** Curated 4-project showcase featuring Skinnovia Dermatology, BGC Urban Residence, Kape Light, and On-Site Fit-Out Execution.
4. **Practice Pillars:**
   - Commercial & Clinical Interiors (Specialized flow, high-trust environments);
   - Urban Condominium Living (Space optimization, bespoke built-ins);
   - Turnkey Fit-Out Management (Hands-on site measurements, contractor coordination).
5. **The Working Process:**
   - Phase 01: Initial Walkthrough & Spatial Diagnostics
   - Phase 02: Layout Development & Lighting Integration
   - Phase 03: Material Sourcing & Showroom Calibration
   - Phase 04: Turnkey On-Site Construction & Fit-Out
6. **Design Ethos & Voice:** Direct quotes from Aaron (*"Talent creates. Character sustains. Every project deserves humility."*).
7. **Inquiry CTA:** Direct invitation to discuss upcoming projects.

### 2. Projects Index (`/projects`)
- Categorized portfolio grid with filters: `All`, `Commercial & Clinic`, `Urban Residential`, `Hospitality`.
- Cards display project photography, client/project name, location, and typology.

- **Skinnovia Dermatology • Laser** (`/projects/skinnovia`): Comprehensive clinical showcase highlighting reception curves, corridor flow, soft indirect lighting, durable finishes, and practitioner ergonomics.
- **Urban Condominium Residence** (`/projects/bgc-condo`): Compact student/urban apartment showing multi-zone sleeping, custom millwork, soft sage and warm oak palette.
- **Kape Light & Lago Cafe** (`/projects/kape-light`): Warm hospitality craft coffee bar and social space.

### 4. About (`/about`)
- Biography & practice introduction strictly adhering to verified facts.
- Aaron's personal commitment to on-site involvement.
- Clear statement of what Aaron Parnala Projects does (Turnkey interior design, space planning, bespoke fit-out) and what it does not do (generic off-the-shelf staging).

### 5. Start a Project / Contact (`/contact`)
- Interactive project qualification form:
  - Client Full Name
  - Email & Preferred Contact Channel
  - Project Typology (Commercial / Clinic, Residential Condo, Hospitality / Cafe, Full Fit-Out)
  - Project Location (Metro Manila area)
  - Estimated Floor Area / Scope
  - Target Timeline
  - Project Description / Design Needs
- Simulated confirmation state explaining that this prototype submission does not transmit live data without Aaron's approval.
